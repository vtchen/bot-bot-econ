2. Scope

This policy covers Bot Bot, a private integration used only by the company owner. It connects to the company's own e-conomic agreement and nothing else.

3. What data is processed

Bookkeeping data from the company's e-conomic agreement:

  • Chart of accounts and entries

  • Products

Bot Bot does not collect data from website visitors and uses no tracking or analytics on these pages. [CONFIRM: hosting of these pages sets no cookies / logs]

Only to help the owner with the company's own bookkeeping: answering her questions about the data and preparing draft journal entries and draft invoices that she reviews and books herself.

7. How long data is kept

  • Data fetched from e-conomic is cached temporarily, for less than 24 hours, and then deleted.

8. Security

  • Access tokens are stored securely as secrets and are never sent in email or chat.
  • Only the company's own agreement is connected, and only the owner uses the integration.
  • Bot Bot creates drafts only; the owner books them herself.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in e-conomic's settings.

9. Rights of individuals

People whose personal data appears in the bookkeeping (for example contacts at customers or suppliers) have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection, subject to legal bookkeeping obligations. Requests can be sent to vee.chen@gmail.com. Complaints can be made to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk.
