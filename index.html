Bot Bot

A private, internal integration between Visma e-conomic and an AI assistant, used only by Twice As Nice ApS.

Bot Bot is not a public app. It is not offered, sold or made available to other companies or e-conomic users. It connects to one e-conomic agreement only: the company's own.

What it is

Bot Bot lets the company owner, Vicky Chen, use an AI assistant (Grok Bot, which runs on a cloud computer) to help with the company's own bookkeeping in e-conomic. The assistant works only on instructions from the owner.

What it does

  • Reads the company's own bookkeeping data: accounts, entries, customers, suppliers, invoices, journals and products.
  • Creates drafts only: draft journal entries and draft invoices.
  • The owner reviews and books every draft herself in e-conomic. Bot Bot does not book entries or send invoices on its own.

Who uses it

Only the owner of Twice As Nice ApS. There are no other users, customers or tenants.

How data is handled (short version)

  • Data is used only to carry out the owner's bookkeeping requests.
  • Data is not shared with third parties, apart from the AI assistant service that processes it to perform the work.
  • Data is not used to train AI models.
  • Data fetched from e-conomic is cached only temporarily, for less than 24 hours.
  • Access tokens are stored securely as secrets and are never sent by email or chat.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in e-conomic's settings.

Details: Privacy policy · Data processing information · Terms of use (EULA)

Connecting and disconnecting

Connect (login): The owner grants access from e-conomic. After access is granted, see the status page.

Disconnect (logout): Remove Bot Bot's access in e-conomic under the settings for apps / integrations on the agreement. Once access is removed, Bot Bot can no longer read or create anything in e-conomic.
