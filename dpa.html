2. Subject and purpose

Helping the company owner with the company's own bookkeeping: reading data from e-conomic and preparing draft journal entries and draft invoices for her to review and book.

3. Types of data

  • Accounts, entries, journals, invoices and products
  • Customer and supplier records, which may include contact details (name, address, email, phone) of business contacts or private individuals

No special categories of personal data (GDPR Art. 9) are intentionally processed.

4. Categories of people

Contacts at customers and suppliers, and any private individuals who are customers or suppliers.

5. Nature of processing

  • Reading data via the e-conomic API
  • Creating draft journal entries and draft invoices (no booking, sending or payment)
  • Temporary caching while a task is carried out

6. Instructions and limits

  • Processing happens only on the owner's instructions.
  • Data is not shared with third parties beyond the AI assistant service processing it.
  • Data is not used to train AI models.
  • Data is not sold or used for any other purpose.

7. Retention and deletion

Cached data is kept for less than 24 hours and then deleted. Bookkeeping records remain in e-conomic. When access is revoked in e-conomic, no further data can be fetched.

8. Security measures

  • Access tokens stored securely as secrets; never sent by email or chat.
  • Single user (the owner) and single agreement (the company's own).
  • Draft-only write access in practice; the owner books everything herself.
  • Access revocable at any time in e-conomic's settings.
